# Search Console, 4 November 2026: impressions fell to almost nothing

Written for the SEO session from a check made in another one. Data from Search Console (last update
4 November); nothing on the site was changed because of it.

## What happened

- 14–27 October: 2,220 impressions, 13 clicks, 257 pages with impressions (about 160 a day).
- 27 October – 2 November: 139 impressions, 2 clicks, 61 pages (about 20 a day).
- The fall is site-wide, not one page: mostly blog articles, the ones that carried the site.

## What it is not

- Not a technical outage. Checked as Googlebot on 4 November: `/`, `/word-to-markdown`,
  `/evernote-to-markdown`, `/blog`, a top article, `/de/…` all 200, no `noindex`, canonicals right,
  hreflang complete, full prerendered text (7,400 words on the Pandoc article).
- robots.txt allows everything but `/open/`, `/open-folder/`, `/report/`, `/api/`, `/history`,
  `/embed`, `/.well-known/`.
- Sitemap: submitted 21 October, last read 27 October, Success, 698 URLs.
- Manual actions: none.
- Not the deploys of 27 October (one FAQ per conversion page, the Trash). The pages that dropped
  were last crawled at the end of September, before any of it.

currently not indexed 18 (validation **failed**), excluded by `noindex` 5, redirect 1.

So Google fetched the pages fine, showed them for a month, and has now chosen not to keep them: a
judgement of value, not an error. Likely reasons, in order:

1. A two-month-old site with few links pointing at it, and about 935 pages, roughly 350 of them
   blog articles in five languages published within weeks — the pattern Google's September 2026
   spam update (24 Sep – 8 Oct, "scaled content") is strictest about.
2. The end of the new-site trial period Google gives fresh content.
3. Unconfirmed ranking volatility reported on forums for late October; no confirmed core update.

## Done on 4 November

Indexing requested (7 of the day's ~10): `/word-to-markdown`, `/confluence-to-markdown`,
`/markdown-to-confluence`, `/html-to-markdown`, `/json-to-markdown`, `/csv-to-markdown`,
`/markdown-to-word`. Not yet: `/obsidian-to-markdown` (inspection hung) and the rest. Requests for
pages Google has just judged and dropped may change nothing until the page itself changes.

## Proposal from the owner: release pages in batches

The owner asked whether to take part of the pages out of the sitemap and put them back 5–10 a week.

- Taking a URL out of the sitemap does not take it out of Google: the 698 URLs are already known and
  are linked from navigation, hreflang and the articles. The sitemap is a hint, not a gate.
- To hold pages back for real they need `noindex` (and to leave the sitemap and the hreflang sets),
  then the `noindex` lifted batch by batch. `QUIET_ENTRIES` already works this way for changelog
  entries; the same switch could cover articles and their translations.
- A batch should be chosen by strength, not by count: first the conversion pages and the English
  articles with impressions before the fall, then a language at a time where that language's pages
  had impressions of their own.
- The aim is fewer, stronger pages in front of Google, plus links from outside — the signal the site
  most lacks.

Decision and implementation belong to the SEO session; nothing above has been changed in the code.

## Decided and done, 4 November (SEO session)

Not batches by count. A page that is kept out of search earns no impressions, so releasing pages on a
timetable proves nothing. Instead, a cut by evidence, from the Search Console pages report for
4 August – 3 November (355 pages with impressions):

- **English articles:** 54 of 70 stay. The 16 with fewer than 20 impressions and no click are out
  (`QUIET_ARTICLES`); 6 of them had no impressions at all.
- **Translations:** 57 of 280 stay, the ones with 10 impressions or a click of their own
  (`INDEXED_TRANSLATIONS`). The other 223 had almost none.
- **Changelog entry pages:** 3 stay, English only (`INDEXED_ENTRIES`: versions, maths, Mermaid). In
  three months every entry page together had earned 11 impressions.
- **Unchanged:** the front page, the 20 conversion pages and the how-to, agents, sharing, API, CLI,
  Action and legal pages, in all five languages.

Pages out of search stay readable and linked. They serve `noindex, follow` and are left out of the
sitemap and the `hreflang` sets (none of the remaining sets points at them).

- Sitemap: 698 → 384 URLs.
- The rules are in `CLAUDE.md` and in `src/lib/article-index.ts`.
- A page comes back by being improved, then moved into its set.

Links from outside go on in their own session (phase 2 of `content/seo-plan.md`).
